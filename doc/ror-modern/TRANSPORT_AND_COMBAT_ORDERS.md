# Transport and combat order corrections

Transport capacity constrains the embarkation commit, not selection or boarding intent. Every eligible actor receives a shore approach; stable entity-ID order breaks simultaneous arrival ties. Passengers beyond capacity remain active, alive and cancellable on shore, and may enter when seats become free. Direct cargo commits still validate capacity atomically.

Unload is a targetable order. The HUD and U enter the same mode; left click chooses the landing point, right click or Escape cancels. Shift may be held when activating U or when clicking the point. A distant target resolves a reachable water mooring with hull clearance, then sails there and unloads. Multiple transports reserve separate moorings through the shared destination reservation index. Cargo mutations happen after the activity update loop; unavailable landing space retries at bounded intervals and retains cargo. Stop and replacement orders clear pending transport state and motion. New ships preserve valid exact positions and check the entire hull when correcting placement. Legacy hull overlaps can retreat gradually through existing overlap cells without crossing new obstructions or teleporting.

Command glyphs have one semantic registry. Verified original glyphs remain for construction, repair, attack, stop, hold, unload and cancellation. Ground fire and each stance have distinct SVG assets. Cargo changes invalidate the selected entity's HUD signature.

Movement recovery measures improvement along the current route leg, including accumulated slow progress. Mere nonzero displacement no longer clears a stall. Replanning the same order keeps the escalation budget; reaching a waypoint and receiving a new order reset it. Terminal failure clears motion, approach reservations and formation membership. Failed work routes terminate through the same policy instead of issuing a fresh path request every tick. The AI's existing bounded, persisted retry policy handles workers and military actors across both skirmish and campaign profiles; local escape choices use known navigation, legal footprints and visible nearby occupancy.

Explicit attack dissolves marching formations and retains pursuit after the chosen enemy dies. Awareness acquires visible hostile units and buildings within sight. Autonomous encounters retain their previous resume behavior. Stop, a new manual order, or a changed stance cancels pursuit; queued manual orders take precedence over retargeting.

Regression coverage includes 64-unit boarding, seat reuse, target selection and both Shift variants, automatic sailing and separate moorings for multiple transports, cancellation, blocked and unreachable landings, checkpoint continuation, orbit detection, slow movement and detours, physical worker recovery, legacy hull retreat, attack chaining, diplomacy and visibility, and distinct command textures. Integration scenarios exercise both native and GDScript movement.
